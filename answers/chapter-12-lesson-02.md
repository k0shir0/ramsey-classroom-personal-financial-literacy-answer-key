# Chapter 12, Lesson 2

## Guided Notes
* Investors can pool their resources together to invest via a **mutual** fund.
* You can usually determine the contents or objective of the fund by its **name**.
* Stocks within a growth stock mutual fund are currently **growing**.
* Increases in the fund's **value** generate your return.
* Mutual funds should be held and left alone for a minimum of **5** years.

## Activity: Comparing Investment Types

| Investment Type | Risk Level | Return Level | Key Advantages | Key Disadvantages |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Money Market Account** | Low | Low | **Low risk** | **Doesn't earn much money** |
| **Single Stocks** | High | Low to High | **Easy to track since the investment is in one company** | **If the company goes bankrupt or fails, there goes all of your money.** |
| **Bonds** | Moderate | Moderate | **U.S. Treasury Bonds are seen as patriotic; better rates than CDs** | **Your money will be held for a certain period of time before you can claim it.** |
| **Mutual Funds** | Moderate | Low to high | **Lower risk than single stocks.** | **Long-term investment; need to leave the money alone for a minimum of five years** |
| **Real Estate** | High | Low to high | **Almost passive income if your renting to others.** | **The owner (in this case you) would have to maintain the property, or if your renting, take on the responsibilities of the house.** |
| **Annuities** | Low | Moderate | **Variable annuities are invested in mutual funds and grow tax-deferred; some guarantee your principal.** | **Fees and premiums for your own investments and money.** |
