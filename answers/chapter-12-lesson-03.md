# Chapter 12, Lesson 3

## Guided Notes
* 401(k): Companies can allow their **employees** to save for retirement.
* 403(b): **nonprofits** can allow their employees to save for retirement.
* Never take out any retirement money **early**.
* The money you put into a **traditional** 401(k) or IRA is tax deferred.
* A **roth** 401(k) or IRA grows tax-free.
* A 401(k) and an IRA are not **investments**. They are a tax-advantage bucket to put investments into.
