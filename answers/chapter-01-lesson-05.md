# Chapter 1, Lesson 5

## Guided Notes
1. The First Foundation: Save **$500** for an emergency fund.
2. The Second Foundation: Get out of debt and **stay** out of debt.
3. The Third Foundation: Pay **cash** for your car.
4. The Fourth Foundation: Pay cash for **collage**.
5. The Fifth Foundation: Build wealth and be outrageously **generous**.
6. Generosity is not just about giving money, it's a **character** quality.
7. Being generous means you are ready to give **more** of something than is expected.
