# Chapter 1 Review — Vocabulary

1. Having a(n) **positive net worth** means the amount of your liabilities is larger than the value of your assets.
2. **Financial literacy** means having the knowledge and skills to manage your personal finances.
3. As a(n) **consumer**, it's up to you to make choices about what you buy and use.
4. **interest** is a fee charged for using borrowed money for a purchase.
5. American culture and businesses have convinced consumers that using **credit** is normal.
6. **personal finance** relates to all of the different money decisions you make each day.
7. Knowing your **net worth** is the starting point for a financial plan.
8. A(n) **asset** is anything you own that has value.
