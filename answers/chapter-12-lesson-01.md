# Chapter 12, Lesson 1

## Guided Notes
* Target investing 15% of your income toward **retirement**.
* The investing process resembles a **marathon**, not a sprint.
* Wealth building requires three ingredients, the first being **money**.
* The second necessary component is **time**.
* The final ingredient involves the **rate** of return.

## Activity: Wealth Building Is Not a Game of Chance

**Part 1: The Lottery**
* $25 weekly lottery spending equals **100** Per Month and **1200** Per Year.
* Spending totals over time at this rate: 10 years: **3000** | 20 years: **6000** | 30 years: **9000** | 40 years: **12000**
* $50 weekly lottery spending equals **200** Per Month and **2400** Per Year.
* Spending totals over time at this rate: 10 years: **24000** | 20 years: **48000** | 30 years: **72000** | 40 years: **96000**
* Net wealth after 40 years of spending $200/month on tickets but winning $100k every 10 years: **$304,000**

**Part 2: Compound Growth Alternative**
* Monthly investment: **100** | Yearly investment: **1200**
* Future value of investing the monthly amount at a 12% return: 10 years: **23,004** | 20 years: **98,925** | 30 years: **349,496** | 40 years: **1,176,476**
* Saving $50 a week yields: **200** Per Month | **2400** Per Year.
* Future value of investing this new monthly amount at a 12% return: 10 years: **46,008** | 20 years: **197,851** | 30 years: **698,992** | 40 years: **2,352,951**
* Future value of taking a one-time investment amount of **200** (representing the monthly lottery cost) at a 12% return: 20 years: **2,179** | 30 years: **7,191**
